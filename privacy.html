Privacy Policy

This website helps people find jobs in Cork, Ireland. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account. If you use our contact form, we receive the details you send us. We store nothing else on our server.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. Google Analytics sets cookies and collects information such as pages viewed, approximate location and device type. You can find out more in Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Advertising

Some pages show advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on your previous visits to this site and other sites. You can manage ad personalisation at google.com/settings/ads.

Facebook

The Facebook page box on job pages is provided by Facebook. Facebook may set its own cookies when you view it.

Favourite jobs

Jobs you save with the heart button are stored only in your own browser (localStorage). We cannot see them.

Donations

Donations are handled by the payment provider's own site. We do not receive or store your payment details.
