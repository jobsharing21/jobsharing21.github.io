Who are we?

JobSharing is a leading global job search platform for listing skills and expertise, allowing employers and employees to connect with each other.

Whether you are looking for a job or a potential employee, you can register to find new opportunities or any freelance jobs and/or remote work in just a few clicks.

JobSharing vision is to be the sharing economy platform for finding a job without any recruitment agencies / headhunters fee in between.

JobSharing mission is to help as many people worldwide find more opportunities and boost their income based on their skills, location and availability.

We make this painful job search process as easy and powerful as possible for everyone.
