Job Sharing Donation

JobSharing as a non-profit organization is proud to be part of the Cork community, providing a job search platform to people from all walks of life and from all over Cork, Ireland.

Job Sharing helps people to find their dream jobs and is run entirely on donations from several different organizations, individuals and corporations. For a few years, Job Sharing has helped dozens of people to land their dream jobs in Cork, Ireland.

If you like our idea, blog posts, and job alerts on our website, please donate to keep our work going.
